Uzbekistan is executing one of Central Asia's most dramatic top-down energy transitions, pivoting from near-total gas dependence toward large-scale solar IPPs financed by Chinese, Saudi, and UAE capital. Vietnam is the book's marquee 'legislate it' case: an aggressive feed-in tariff manufactured a credible offtake commitment and triggered one of the world's fastest solar booms. It shows how a domestic policy instrument can stand in for external anchoring — and how FIT design choices then transfer the strain onto grid integration and curtailment.
